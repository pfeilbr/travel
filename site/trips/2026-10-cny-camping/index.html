Gotchas

  • Fees: ReserveAmerica rates exclude the reservation fee ($7.25 for the first night) and the park vehicle fee. Same-day reservations close at 3 pm. Walk-ins are allowed for campsites only.
  • Green Lakes cabins: the park website says 2-night minimum, but ReserveAmerica priced cabin #001 at "Use Fees (1 night) $115.50" with no minimum-stay warning. 1 night looks bookable; confirm at checkout.
  • Sampson: the park cottages have a 2-night minimum from Sep 10 to Oct 12, so the 3 listed at $300 probably won't book for 1 night. Seneca Lake Resorts (Campspot) has nothing for 1 night, but 31 cabins are open for Oct 4–6 (2 nights) at about $172–194/night. Camping closes Oct 11.
  • Watkins Glen: no cabins available (they also have a 3-night minimum). Weekends only after Sep 8; Sunday night still showed open sites. Closes Oct 11.
  • Glimmerglass: the park lists a 2-night minimum, but a 1-night search still returned 21 sites. Closes Oct 12.
  • Closing dates on ReserveAmerica: Keuka, Nicks Lake, Sampson and Watkins Glen Oct 11; Glimmerglass Oct 12; Selkirk Shores Oct 24; Treman Nov 7; Moreau Nov 28.
  • Firelight: only tents 16–19 have electric heaters, and early-October nights can be in the 40s, so ask for one of those. Dogs cost $50 extra (non-electric tents only). Late checkout costs $50.

Top 3

  1. Robert H. Treman: the cheapest cabin of any option ($80.50, 6 open), waterfall gorge hike, 10 minutes from Ithaca restaurants and bars. Firelight is next door if you'd rather glamp.
  2. Green Lakes: the closest at ~45 min, 3 cabins open, good biking and trout fishing, with Syracuse restaurants nearby. Easiest for a Sunday-to-Monday trip.
  3. Selkirk Shores: 1 cabin left ($128), Lake Ontario sunsets, and the Salmon River run is at its peak, which makes it the best fishing pick.
